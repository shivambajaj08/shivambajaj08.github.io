Students learn to model dynamic systems, analyze stability and transient response, and design classical and state-space controllers through analytical derivations and simulation labs.

### Key Topics & Components
* **Modeling & Frequency/Root-Locus Design:** Transfer functions, stability margins, root locus construction, and compensator design.
* **Hands-On Labs:** Simulation and verification exercises connecting mathematical models to physical system behavior.
